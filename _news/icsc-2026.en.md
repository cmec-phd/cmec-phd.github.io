At the 8th International Csound Conference (Trapani, 20–22 October 2026), [Francesco Vitucci](/en/people/francesco-vitucci/) presents the paper [*One Timeline, Many Renderings: A Wolfram Language Paclet for Heterogeneous Musical Output*](/en/research/vitucci2026timeline/).

On the same day, at 15:00, the conference concert programme features a performance of Francesco Vitucci's *Lumina*.
