A seminar marking the centenary of the “N. Piccinni” Conservatory, the Apulian Academy of Sciences, and the University of Bari “Aldo Moro”.

[Francesco Scagliola](/en/people/francesco-scagliola/) and [Francesco Vitucci](/en/people/francesco-vitucci/) present the Piccinni Conservatory’s PhD programme in “Computational Electroacoustic Music Composition, Generative Algorithmic Models and Computational Complexity”.
