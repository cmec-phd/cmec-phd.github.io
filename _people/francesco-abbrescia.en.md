Trained in piano, voice and choral conducting, he holds diplomas in Experimental Composition and Electronic Music from the Bari Conservatory. He directed the vocal ensemble Goitre (2007-2011) and the contemporary music series «Musiche Nuove» (2004-2011). He performs internationally as a digital music performer and won the 2008/09 National Arts Prize for Electronic Music. He is a member of Sin[x]Thésis, the Conservatory's electronic music research and production group.
