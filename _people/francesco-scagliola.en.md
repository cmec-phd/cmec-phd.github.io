Composer, sound artist, music theorist, and academic (b. 1971).

A graduate in piano, composition, and electronic music, he pursued advanced studies with Azio Corghi at the Accademia Nazionale di Santa Cecilia and with Luciano Berio at Tempo Reale. His work spans instrumental and electronic music as well as intermedia arts, and has been presented at the Venice Music Biennale, Settimana Musicale Senese, Rai Radio 3, and international festivals. He has collaborated with artists including the Arditti Quartet, Luis Bacalov, Luca Francesconi, Giacomo Manzoni, and Steve Reich.

His research focuses on computational models for generative music and multimedia, with contributions published internationally and in the Springer volume *Imagine Math*.

He is Professor of Electroacoustic Music Composition at the “N. Piccinni” Conservatory in Bari, where he coordinates the Electronic Music programmes and the PhD programme in Electroacoustic Music Computation, Generative Algorithmic Models and Computational Complexity. His students have received six Italian National Arts Awards. From 2002 to 2014, he served as Assistant Maestro at the Accademia Musicale Chigiana.
