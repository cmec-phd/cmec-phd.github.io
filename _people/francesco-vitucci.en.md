Composer, electroacoustic performer, and researcher based in Bari.

He graduated in Composition, Technological Musical Disciplines, and Electronic Music at the “N. Piccinni” Conservatory in Bari, and specialised at the Conservatory of Italian Switzerland in Lugano and at the Accademia Nazionale di Santa Cecilia in Rome, always with full marks and honours, under Biagio Putignano, Nadir Vassena, Ivan Fedele, Michelangelo Lupone, and Francesco Scagliola.

A member of S.I.M.C. (Italian Society of Contemporary Music), he has had his works performed in Italy and abroad and has received commissions from, among others, the ICO Symphony Orchestra of the Metropolitan City of Bari, Orchestra della Magna Grecia, the ICO Sanremo Symphony Orchestra, ArteScienza, and the Traetta Opera Festival. His awards include First Prize at the “2 Agosto” International Composition Competition (2021), the Jurypreis and Publikumspreis at the Junges Lied-Wettbewerb in Zurich (2022), the Grand Prix at “New Music Generation 2022”, a finalist place at the “Luciano Berio” Competition (2023), selection at ICMC 2024 in Seoul, and the Goffredo Petrassi Award from the President of the Italian Republic. His works are published by SZ Sugar, Stradivarius, Taukay, and Da Vinci Publishing.

His doctoral research focuses on artificial memory, the computational modelling of musical form, roughness as a parameter for analysing musical recurrences, and ontological models of musical time, with contributions presented at the Colloquium on Music Informatics (CIM) and the International Csound Conference.

Since 2020 he has taught the Music Analysis seminar for the Master of Arts in Orchestral Conducting at the Conservatory of Italian Switzerland; between 2023 and 2024 he taught Composition at the “G. Rossini” Conservatory in Pesaro.
